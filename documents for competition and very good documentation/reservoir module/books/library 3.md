Here is the complete bibliographic list of every book referenced across the tool-suite guide, organised by the functional modules of a comprehensive reservoir engineering software suite.

### 🧱 Core Reservoir Engineering & Physics
*   **Fundamentals of Reservoir Engineering** by L.P. Dake. Elsevier, 1978 (Volume 8; later corrected editions available).
*   **The Practice of Reservoir Engineering (Revised Edition)** by L.P. Dake. Elsevier, 2001 (Revised ed.; original 1994).
*   **Reservoir Engineering Handbook** by Tarek Ahmed. Gulf Professional Publishing, 2019 (5th ed.).
*   **Integrated Reservoir Engineering: From Fundamentals to Field Development** by D. Nathan Meehan. CRC Press, forthcoming January 2027.
*   **The Properties of Petroleum Fluids** by William D. McCain, Jr. PennWell Books, 1990 (2nd ed.; a 3rd ed. was also released later).
*   **Gas Reservoir Engineering** by John Lee and Robert A. Wattenbarger. Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE), 1996.

### ⚙️ Numerical Simulation & Algorithms
*   **An Introduction to Reservoir Simulation Using MATLAB/GNU Octave: User Guide for the MATLAB Reservoir Simulation Toolbox (MRST)** by Knut-Andreas Lie. Cambridge University Press, 2019.
*   **Advanced Modelling with the MATLAB Reservoir Simulation Toolbox (MRST)** edited by Knut-Andreas Lie and Olav Møyner. Cambridge University Press, 2021.
*   **Fundamentals of Numerical Reservoir Simulation** by Donald W. Peaceman. Elsevier Scientific Publishing Company, 1977.
*   **Basic Applied Reservoir Simulation** by Turgay Ertekin, Jamal H. Abou-Kassem, and Gregory R. King. Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE), 2001 (SPE Textbook Series, Vol. 7).
*   **Principles of Applied Reservoir Simulation** by John R. Fanchi. Gulf Professional Publishing, 2018 (4th ed.).

### 🗺️ Geostatistics & Geological Modeling
*   **Geostatistical Reservoir Modeling** by Michael J. Pyrcz and Clayton V. Deutsch. Oxford University Press, 2014 (2nd ed.).
*   **Applied Geostatistics in Python: A Hands-on Guide with GeostatsPy** by Michael J. Pyrcz. 2024 (Free e-book, Zenodo).
*   **Geostatistics: Modeling Spatial Uncertainty** by Jean-Paul Chilès and Pierre Delfiner. Wiley, 2012 (2nd ed.).

### 📈 Well Testing & Pressure Transient Analysis
*   **Modern Pressure Transient Analysis of Petroleum Reservoirs: A Practical View** by Tarek Al Arbi Omar Ganat. Springer, 2023.
*   **Pressure Transient Testing** by John Lee, John B. Rollins, and John P. Spivey. Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE), 2003 (SPE Textbook Series, Vol. 9).
*   **Modern Well Test Analysis: A Computer-Aided Approach** by Roland N. Horne. Petroway, 1995 (1st ed.; subsequent editions exist).

### 🏭 Production Engineering & Optimization
*   **Petroleum Production Engineering: A Computer-Assisted Approach** by Boyun Guo, William C. Lyons, and Al Guo. Gulf Professional Publishing, 2007 (and later editions).
*   **Production Optimization of Oil and Gas Fields Using NeqSim: From Reservoir to Market — Theory, Methods, and Applications** by Even Solbraa. Equinor ASA / NTNU, 2026 (1st revised ed.).

### 💰 Petroleum Economics & Risk Analysis
*   **Fundamentals of Applied Reservoir Engineering: Appraisal, Economics and Optimization** by Richard Wheaton. Gulf Professional Publishing, 2016.
*   **Practical Enhanced Reservoir Engineering: Assisted with Simulation Software** by Abdus Satter, Ghulam M. Iqbal, and James L. Buchwalter. PennWell Corp., 2008.
*   **Petroleum Engineering & Economics Essentials** by Eastex Petroleum Consultants (companion book to the PE Essentials software suite; see publisher's site for current edition details).

### 🤖 Machine Learning & Data Analytics
*   **Machine Learning in the Oil and Gas Industry: Including Geosciences, Reservoir Engineering, and Production Engineering with Python** by Yogendra Narayan Pandey, Ayush Rastogi, Sribharath Kainkaryam, Srimoyee Bhattacharya, and Luigi Saputelli. Apress, 2020.
*   **Applied Machine Learning in Python: A Hands-on Guide with Code** by Michael J. Pyrcz. 2024 (Free e-book, Zenodo).
*   **Python for Oil & Gas: Data Science, Engineering Analytics, and AI in the Petroleum Industry** by Johnpaul Okeke. 2026 (1st ed.).

### 💻 Software Architecture & Python Implementation
*   **Mastering Petroleum Engineering with Python: From Physics to Artificial Intelligence** by Jamie Flux. Independently published, 2024.
*   **Software Architecture with Python: Design and Architect Highly Scalable, Robust, Clean, and High Performance Applications in Python** by Anand Balachandran Pillai. Packt Publishing, 2017.

### 🚀 Modern & Specialized Topics
*   **Modern Reservoir Engineering Handbook: A Practical Guide to Simulation, Unconventional Reservoirs, History Matching, and CO₂ Storage** by Sienna Newman. Independently published, 2026.
*   **SPEE Monograph 4: Estimating Developed Reserves in Unconventional Reservoirs** by the Society of Petroleum Evaluation Engineers (SPEE). 2015.
*   **Advanced Petroleum Reservoir Simulation: Towards Developing Reservoir Emulators** by M. Rafiqul Islam et al. Wiley/Scrivener, 2016 (2nd ed.; 1st ed. 2010).

**Note on availability:** A few titles are forthcoming (Meehan, 2027) or have limited distribution (the Eastex companion book, some e-books), so you may need to check the publisher or author’s site directly for purchase or access.
